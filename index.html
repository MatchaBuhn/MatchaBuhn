FAQ

How long does shipping take?

This is a digital product, so there is no physical shipping. Your Sticker Pack is available for download after your purchase is completed through Gumroad.

What sizes are available?

The Sticker Pack includes transparent PNG sticker files. Sizes are XS — 2" × 2", S — 3" × 3", M — 4" × 4", and L — 5.5" × 5.5". The stickers are ready for digital use after purchase, as well as physical printing and cutting.

Do you accept returns?

Digital products are delivered instantly after purchase in your digital files, meaning all digital sales are final. Please review the product details before purchasing.
